\section{Software environment}

Python 3.12.14; RDKit 2026.09.1; scikit-learn 1.9.1; NumPy 2.5.4; SciPy 1.18.1; pandas 3.1.0; matplotlib 3.11.2. The full pipeline (\texttt{fetch\_data.py}, \texttt{run\_predictions.py}, \texttt{analyse.py}, \texttt{make\_figures.py}, \texttt{make\_tables.py}) runs in about 40 minutes on an 18-core laptop CPU. Input digests (SHA-256) are listed in \texttt{PROVENANCE.md} and checked by \texttt{fetch\_data.py}.
