\section{Analysis plan}

The plan below is a historical record reproduced in its original wording, not a revised claim of statistical validity. Protocol 1.0 was committed at \texttt{f187a12}; note 1.1 at \texttt{0269f67}; pre-scoring analysis code at \texttt{feadf9a}. The timing rests on local repository history from the same working session, not an external registration. Later documentation and bootstrap corrections are recorded in note 1.2 of \texttt{PROTOCOL.md}. Notes on implementation follow the historical plan.

For the 8 November revision, statements in the original plan about a threshold guarantee are superseded by the empirical interpretation in the main manuscript. Candidates and their order use calibration data, and no finite-sample guarantee is established. The original conformal-style normalizer uses the calibration fold, its quantile convention is retained, and neither marginal coverage for this implementation nor accepted-subset coverage under shift is established. The random/held-out Gate~B comparison refits the scorer and changes threshold data and sample size. The hypotheses, pass criteria and original calculations are retained.

The cards are descriptive summaries of accepted prediction--seed observations. A compound or reaction may repeat across seeds and related chemistry shares groups; the original pooled Wilson intervals were removed. Tables S5--S8 give per-seed precision, correct/accepted counts and coverage. No replacement interval or inference to unseen chemical groups/campaigns is claimed. The original H1 query-bootstrap intervals describe the retained analysis conditional on fitted pipelines, with incomplete chemical-group dependence. In logP $H$ and SM $H$, partly overlapping query sets are resampled independently across seeds, so repeated-query dependence is not fully preserved. Statistical review of the target population and paired/clustered sampling design is needed before a new inferential analysis. E1 and E2 share 182 compounds; BH tests share a campaign. The eight tests are not independent replications.

\section{Software environment}

Python 3.12.14; RDKit 2026.09.1; scikit-learn 1.9.1; NumPy 2.5.4; SciPy 1.18.1; pandas 3.1.0; matplotlib 3.11.2. The locked dependencies are installed with \texttt{uv sync --frozen --python 3.12}. The full pipeline (\texttt{fetch\_data.py}, \texttt{run\_predictions.py}, \texttt{analyse.py}, \texttt{make\_figures.py}, \texttt{make\_tables.py}) runs in about 40 minutes on an 18-core laptop CPU. Input digests (SHA-256) are listed in \texttt{PROVENANCE.md} and checked by \texttt{fetch\_data.py}. M3 (amendment 2.0) runs in a separate environment, because Chemprop requires an older RDKit: Chemprop 2.3.1, PyTorch 2.15.0, Lightning 2.6.6, RDKit 2026.03.6 (\texttt{env/graph-requirements.txt}). Its 35 fits (seven tasks, five seeds, five members each) ran on the CPU, six jobs at a time with three threads each (\texttt{scripts/graph\_jobs.py}); the main environment computes the splits, the similarity signals, the gates and the metrics as for M1 and M2.

\section{Per-seed descriptive operating results}
These tables extend the descriptive reporting in this revision; they do not change the pre-specified hypothesis criteria. Coverage includes zero-acceptance seeds. Undefined precision is not replaced by zero or one. Counts are correct/accepted predictions within each seed; repeated queries across seeds remain paired observations, not independent trials. The machine-readable files \texttt{results/revision/gate\_per\_seed.csv} and \texttt{gate\_pooled\_descriptive.csv} also give test counts and family labels. H2 retains its original convention that an all-abstaining cell meets the target vacuously.
